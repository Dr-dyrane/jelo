# Safety, evidence, rights, and privacy

## Care-first override

Flag `burning`, `swelling`, `rash`, `severe symptoms`, `misinformation`,
`privacy risk`, and `buying intent` explicitly.

For irritation, use supportive, non-diagnostic language. Suggest stopping the
suspected product for now. Ask what was used and what symptoms appeared only
when useful. Recommend urgent medical help for trouble breathing, eye
involvement, severe or rapidly worsening swelling, widespread blistering, or
other severe symptoms. Do not prescribe, diagnose, guarantee, or sell through
distress.

Do not repeat a person's address, phone, order number, medical history, or other
sensitive detail in public. Move private consultation carefully without asking
them to expose more information in the thread.

## Commerce evidence

Before a product, retailer, price, stock, or delivery statement, resolve:

- exact brand and product name;
- exact variant and size;
- seller/retailer identity;
- current source URL;
- checked-at time;
- price basis and delivery exclusion/inclusion;
- whether the compared offers are genuinely like-for-like.

If any essential field is missing, narrow the statement or ask for it. A product
spotlight may omit comparison prices. Never imply authenticity, partnership,
endorsement, availability, or performance without evidence.

Use exact approved packshots. Do not redraw a pack, invent its label, make a
container float, or generate a deceptive lookalike.

## Rights states

- `owned` — JeloCare owns the asset and may use it within the recorded scope.
- `licensed` — permission and scope are documented.
- `platform-native-only` — use only through the original platform's native
  surface; do not download and cross-post.
- `unknown` — analysis/private draft only.

Ownership or a licence is not enough by itself. For a renderer to mark an asset
ready for public approval, record `scope: public-channel-authorized` and name
the exact permitted channels in `authorizedChannels`. Private-only or
platform-native-only scope remains `local-private-unpublished`.

Public accessibility is not a reuse licence. A user-supplied file is evidence,
not automatic permission. Record creator, performer, music/audio, and platform
rights separately when relevant.

## Provenance

For every retained asset, record original path or source URL, received/checked
time, dimensions/duration, SHA-256, permitted use, transformations, and whether
the raw source is retained. Never put credentials, cookies, tokens, or private
customer data in provenance files.
