# Publishing, profile, and measurement

## Exact action unit

Approval binds one platform/action, target URL, exact copy, exact media file and
SHA-256, and checked-at state. Changing any member invalidates it.

Resolve → preview → explicit confirmation → submit → live verification →
receipt. Do not combine likes, reposts, follows, DMs, downloads, cross-posts, or
spend with approval for a reply.

The live receipt records public URL, external ID, exact visible copy, media
presence/hash where observable, reply ancestry, published-at time, verification
time, and initial visible metrics. If target-level proof fails, use
`submitted-unverified`.

## Article and profile system

Use the profile as a small conversion path:

- pinned post: fast first action or strongest converter;
- Highlights: durable article or guide;
- replies: relevance, trust, care, and discovery;
- product/retailer spotlights: specific commerce proof;
- website: comparison, consultation, contribution, and purchase routes.

An X article should be concise, skimmable, and useful before any call to action.
Derive WhatsApp/Instagram hooks from one tension or number in the article. Keep
the visual hook clean; put the real link in the caption or platform link
surface, not in a fake button.

## Measurement

Prefer planned observations near 1 h, 6 h, and 24 h. Record the time actually
observed rather than backfilling. Public views are cumulative impressions, not
unique people or revenue.

Separate:

- source-post reach;
- JeloCare reply reach and engagement;
- author response;
- audience continuation;
- profile/website outcomes, only when owned analytics exist.

Compare cohorts by mechanism, source fit, timing, and package. Do not infer
causality from a single reply attached to a viral source.
