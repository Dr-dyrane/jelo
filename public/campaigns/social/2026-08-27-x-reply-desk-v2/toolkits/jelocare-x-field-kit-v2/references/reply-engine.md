# Reply engine

## Candidate intake

Capture exact public evidence when the host can browse:

- stable URL/post ID;
- author handle and display name;
- exact visible text and visible time;
- checked-at UTC timestamp;
- replies, reposts, likes, bookmarks, and views as visible;
- source media type and whether audio exists;
- whether the author is actively replying;
- exact post/reply that JeloCare would answer;
- current JeloCare reply/duplicate state;
- safety, privacy, buying-intent, claim, and rights flags.

When browsing is unavailable, preserve the same fields from the supplied
screenshot and mark the live-only fields `unknown`.

## Priority

Prefer a fresh source with active conversation, a clear skincare/commerce/care
opening, and a reply that adds a second beat. Prioritize the first two hours,
but do not force weak work merely because it is fresh. A smaller strong thread
can beat a large dead one.

Reject spam, engagement bait with no JeloCare angle, generic reactions, unsafe
health jokes, stale replies, unclear ancestry, already-answered targets, and
sources whose value depends on copying unlicensed media elsewhere.

## Five lanes

- `answer promptly` — a clear question, correction, or useful factual opening.
- `light banter` — a source-fit second beat with no care or commerce pressure.
- `care/safety response` — irritation, symptoms, misuse, or harmful advice.
- `buying intent` — product, size, price, stock, shop, delivery, or consultation
  intent.
- `ignore` — no worthwhile or safe contribution.

## Follow-ups

Track stable reply IDs already reported. On each run:

1. inspect direct replies/comments on JeloCare posts and replies;
2. compare against reported IDs and prior visible text;
3. surface only new substantive items or meaningful edits/engagement changes;
4. suppress unchanged items, spam, and emoji-only reactions;
5. draft one response only when warranted.

## Naturalness review

Ask:

- Who or what is the sentence actually about?
- Does every pronoun point to the correct person or object?
- Is the joke answering the full thread story, including JeloCare's earlier
  reply when this is a follow-up?
- Would this line still work without the brand avatar?
- Does it invite a response rather than close the room?

Do not post during review.
