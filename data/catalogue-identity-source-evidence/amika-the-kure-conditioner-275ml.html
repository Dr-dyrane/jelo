what is the kure strength repair conditioner?

a bond fortifying conditioner that's ultra moisturizing + clinically proven to strengthen damaged strands due to everyday stressors, heat + chemical treatments.

who it's for

all hair types 1–4c experiencing damage
best for high porosity hair

why it's special

this conditioner intensely hydrates while working to strengthen + bring dry, damaged hair back from its breaking point.

  • claims - 55% less breakage* + 2x stronger* hair after one use.
  • safe for strands: clinically proven to be safe for color-treated hair.**

*after 1 use when used as a system, clinically proven.

**clinically tested when shampoo + conditioner are used as a system.

clinically tested results
  • claims - 55% less breakage* + 2x stronger* hair after one use.
  • safe for strands: clinically proven to be safe for color-treated hair.**

*after 1 use when used as a system, clinically proven.

**clinically tested when shampoo + conditioner are used as a system.

how to use?
  1. squeeze excess water out of hair.
  2. apply on mid-lengths to ends. use comb to detangle + evenly disperse product.
  3. leave on for 2-3 minutes.
  4. rinse thoroughly.

liter pumps how-to:
the pumps will need to be opened/unlocked prior to putting them in your liter-sized bottles -- these pumps have a tight lock on them (so that you can re-lock if needed for travel or storage, and be assured nothing will leak)!

  1. please hold the dip tube (straw like portion) in one hand, just below where the pump sticks out from under the threaded cap, and place your other hand on the top of the pump (that dispenses product).
  2. twist the hand on the top portion of the pump (where product dispenses) away from you, and the hand on the dip tube/just under the threaded portion of the lid towards you.

safety information:

avoid contact with eyes.

ingredients
key ingredients
  • bond fortifying technology: helps target + strengthen the bonds
    in hair most prone to breakage due to environmental stressors, heat, chemical treatments, + more.
  • plant butters + vegan proteins: formulated with both plant butters + vegan proteins, the kure is rich in nutrients like omega fatty acids, vitamins + minerals + can provide keratin-like benefits from a plant source.
  • sea buckthorn: known as 1 of earth’s most potent, omega-rich plant sources, this superfruit berry nourishes strands, scalp + skin while restoring lipids in the hair to help increase shine, manageability, softness + strength.
